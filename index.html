Our Story

A neighborhood ritual,
brewed with intention.

We started Brew & Bean in a tiny corner shop with one second-hand espresso machine and a stubborn belief: great coffee shouldn't be complicated. Twelve years later, we still roast in small batches every Tuesday, still greet regulars by name, and still believe the best conversations happen over a warm cup.

  • ✓ Roasted in-house, twice a week
  • ✓ Direct-trade relationships with farmers
  • ✓ Compostable cups & zero-waste packaging
